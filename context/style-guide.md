# Style Guide — Iron Steel

## Brand Colors

### Primary — Gris Métal
- Main: `#4A4A4A`
- Light: `#888888`
- Dark: `#2F2F2F`

### Accent — Doré
- Main: `#B8860B`
- Light: `#D4A84A`
- Dark: `#9A7009`

### Neutral
- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Surface: `#F7F7F7`
- Border: `#E8E8E8`

---

## Typography

### Font Family
```
Poppins (Google Fonts)
- Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700
```

### Headings
- Font: Poppins
- Weight: 600
- Color: `#0F0F0F`

### Body
- Font: Poppins
- Weight: 400
- Color: `#4A4A4A`

---

## Components

### Border Radius
- Small: 4px
- Medium: 8px (boutons, inputs)
- Large: 12px (cards)

### Shadows
- Style: Subtiles
- Usage: Cards au hover, modals

### Transitions
- Duration: 200ms
- Easing: ease-out

---

## Images

### Galeries Prestations
- Ratio: 4:3 ou 16:9
- Qualité: Haute résolution
- Style: Photos réelles des réalisations

### Icônes
- Library: Lucide React
- Size: 20px (inline), 24px (buttons), 32px (features)
- Color: Inherit

---
